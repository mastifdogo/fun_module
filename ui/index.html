Desk Buddy

A little friend who keeps you company while you do the books.

What she does

  • Wanders along the bottom of the screen, peeks at things and follows your cursor with her eyes.
  • Climbs the sides of windows (side-by-side panes, cards and dialogs) and sits on top of them, legs dangling.
  • Close a pane or dialog she's on, or scroll her card away, and she falls to whatever is below — usually the bottom of the screen.
  • Open a new pane and she'll often run over to climb it.
  • Watches while you type, waves when your cursor hangs around, and falls asleep when you stop working.
  • Drag her anywhere and let go to throw her. Click to poke, double-click for a heart.

Keeping her around

She lives in this Ledger browser tab until it's reloaded. To have her come back by herself, open Desk Buddy beside (the button next to it in the sidebar) and make that pane narrow: Ledger restores panes on reload, and she comes back out.

Her name, look, size, energy and bedtime are under Settings → Desk Buddy.
